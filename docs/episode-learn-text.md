## 1. Chike Explores the Solar System
**The discovery:** Planets further from the sun take much longer to travel around it. Earth takes one year to go all the way around the sun, but faraway Neptune takes about 165 of our years! The farther out a planet is, the longer its path around the sun.

**The life lesson:** Curiosity is worth following, even when the question is enormous. Chike turns a desk-top model of the planets into a whole trip through space. Big questions get easier when you explore them one step at a time.

## 5. Kiyah's Bee-utiful Drawing

**The life lesson:** Looking closely at something ordinary is its own kind of discovery. When the bees won't hold still, a magnifying glass helps Kiyah see details she never noticed before, and her drawing changes. Slowing down to look closely can make everyday things amazing.
