# Episode 11: A Story You Can Sing

Runtime: 2 minutes 30 seconds. Cast: Chike, Yuna and Stacey. Setting: Chike's treehouse work studio.
Creative focus: rhyme, poetry, and turning words into a song.

Seedance 2.5, 16:9, 720p. Rendered one scene per clip (each scene is 17 to 23 seconds; the poem is split in two),
starting every prompt with the series prompt block. References: the character sheets plus episode clips only
(Ep1/Ep2 for Chike's self-moving chair and stools, Ep9 for Yuna, Stacey's own clips for her voice).

**Rules:** no glowing eyes, ever. Stacey has a calm, more mature voice. In dialogue spell Yuna "Yoona" and Chike
"Cheekay". Chike's office chair and stools roll out on their own.

---

## 00:00–00:20 | Stacey's Lyrics

The friends are walking into the doorway of the treehouse studio. Warm afternoon light fills the treehouse studio.
Chike's stools and office chair automatically roll from under the table. They all take a seat. Yuna and Chike lean
over a page of lyrics. Stacey sits across from them with a pencil.

YUNA:
"You bring the beat, I'll bring the rhyme.
We'll make a little music, one line at a time."

Yuna looks up, smiling.

YUNA: Stacey, I love this! The words fit together.

CHIKE: I can almost hear the song already.

STACEY: That's the idea!

## 00:20–00:43 | A Different Kind of Story

STACEY: Yoona, you should write a song.

YUNA: Me? I write stories, not songs.

Chike leans forward. A glowing question mark appears beside his head.

CHIKE: Are you going to create a song today?

YUNA: I wouldn't know where to start, Cheekay.

STACEY: Start with a story. Or better yet, a poem with a melody.

YUNA: A poem with a melody…

Yuna turns to a blank page.

## 00:43–01:00 | Finding the First Line

Yuna looks around the studio: a pencil, a paper boat, clouds beyond the window.

YUNA: So I could write about anything?

STACEY: Anything you want to say.

CHIKE: What's on your mind?

YUNA: How a little idea can become something bigger.

Yuna begins writing. A soft, colorful imagination cloud swirl curls around the table and fills the frame. The friends
remain absorbed in her words, unaware of the change.

## 01:00–01:35 | Yuna's Poem

The clouds part to reveal a blue ribbon river winding through a landscape of paper illustrations. Yuna's voice continues.

YUNA:
A little boat, a folded sheet,
A tiny sail, a captain's seat.

A paper boat unfolds on the river.

YUNA:
A pencil draws a winding stream,
And sends my boat into a dream.

A pencil line curves ahead of the boat. Paper hills rise along the banks.

YUNA:
Past button hills and cotton snow,
Past pinwheel fields that spin and blow.

Buttons become rolling hills. Cotton clouds drift overhead. Colorful pinwheels turn.

YUNA:
I start with something small and new,
Then wonder what my hands can do.

Paper pieces gather into a miniature town.

YUNA:
One little thought, one thing to try—
Who knows how far my boat could fly?

The boat's sail opens into paper wings. It glides toward a treehouse-shaped cloud.

## 01:35–01:53 | "She's Really Good"

The colorful imagination cloud swirl curls across the landscape. It clears to reveal the paper boat resting on the
studio table.

Yuna finishes writing. Chike and Stacey stare at her, surprised. No one acknowledges the transition.

YUNA: Was that… too much?

Chike and Stacey turn toward each other.

CHIKE: She's good.

STACEY: She's really good.

Yuna laughs, a little embarrassed.

YUNA: It was just a story.

STACEY: And I could picture every line.

## 01:53–02:13 | Giving It a Melody

Stacey gently taps a steady beat on the table.

STACEY: Listen.

She sings a simple melody using Yuna's opening lines.

STACEY, SINGING:
"A little boat, a folded sheet,
A tiny sail, a captain's seat."

Yuna's expression brightens.

YUNA: Those are my words!

CHIKE: Now they have a tune.

STACEY: We can repeat a favorite line, too.

YUNA: "One little thought, one thing to try."

## 02:13–02:30 | Their Song Begins

CHIKE: Let's work on a song together.

STACEY: You bring the story.

YUNA: You bring the melody.

Chike picks up two pencils and taps a gentle rhythm.

CHIKE: I'll bring the beat.

They pull their chairs closer. Yuna writes, Stacey hums, and Chike keeps time.

ALL, SINGING SOFTLY: (they all laugh)

The camera pulls back through the treehouse window as the friends continue working on their song.

Fade out.
