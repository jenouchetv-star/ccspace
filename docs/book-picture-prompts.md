# Book pictures to make in Higgsfield

Six books each have one spread (two facing pages) with no picture. One new picture per book fills it.

**For every picture:**
- **Shape:** wide, 3:2 landscape. It fills the bottom half of the page, under the text.
- **Style:** the same as the existing book pages, like the floating-train pictures in Mateo's book: bright, bold-outlined cartoon, warm light, expressive faces.
- **Reference:** attach each character's character sheet from your Higgsfield library, so they look like themselves.
- **No text:** no words or letters in the picture. The book already has the text.

When they're done, tell me and I'll find them in your Higgsfield history, crop them and place them on the right pages.
