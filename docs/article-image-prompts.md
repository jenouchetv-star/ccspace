# Picture prompts for the nine News & Articles drafts

Each article needs two pictures:

- **Cover**: shown on the article's card and beside its title. Make it landscape, 4:3.
- **Photo**: shown inside the article where its `[photo]` line sits. Make it landscape, 3:2.

Add them in **Admin > Articles > (the article)**: **Cover image** and **Photo in the article**. Each one comes with suggested alt text (the description screen readers read aloud); change it to match the picture you get.

Add this to the end of every prompt, so the set looks consistent:

> Natural, warm documentary photo style, soft daylight, real everyday setting, diverse children aged 5 to 11, candid (not posed for the camera), no text, no logos, no brand names on any device or clothing.

---

## 1. Fewer kids are reading for fun

**Cover:** A child of about 9 curled up in a cozy reading nook by a window, completely absorbed in a chapter book, afternoon light, a small stack of library books beside them.
Alt: *A child curled up by a window, absorbed in a chapter book.*

**Photo:** A parent and a 6-year-old sharing a picture book on a couch, the child pointing at a page and laughing.
Alt: *A parent and a young child sharing a picture book on a couch.*
