What did they learn?

The discovery

Water evaporates into the sky, cools into clouds, and falls back down again as rain.

How it works New

1Evaporation

The sun warms the puddle, and the water slowly turns into an invisible gas called water vapor that floats up.

2Condensation

High in the sky the air is cold, so the vapor cools and gathers into tiny drops. Billions of them make a cloud.

3Precipitation

When the drops in a cloud get big and heavy, they fall back down as rain, and the journey starts again.

Words to know New

evaporateto change from a liquid into a gas
condenseto cool from a gas back into tiny drops
precipitationwater falling from clouds, like rain or snow

The life lesson

Everyone's talent, whether storytelling, art or dance, adds something different to one shared idea.

Talk about it New

  • Where do you think the water from a puddle goes on a sunny day?
  • Why do clouds form high in the sky and not down by the ground?
  • What is your talent, and how could it help a friend's idea?

Try it at home New

  1. Put a little warm water in a clear zip bag, seal it and tape it to a sunny window.
  2. Check it after an hour: drops form near the top (condensation).
  3. Watch them run back down, like rain.
