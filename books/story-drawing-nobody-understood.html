“My picture will never look right,” Kylah whispered.

2

Chike noticed her frown. “What are you creating today?” he asked. “I am trying to draw the garden,” Kylah said, “but everything keeps moving.”

3

“Maybe the garden is giving you clues,” Chike said.

4

They looked closely together — yellow pollen on a bee, tiny holes in a leaf, a spider web shining like silver. Kylah drew one clue at a time.

5

By evening her page held a bee, a leaf, a ladybug, a web, and bright flowers — the garden she had discovered up close.
