    Build with your hand

    This game uses computer vision: an AI looks through your camera and finds 21 points on your hand. Pinch your thumb and pointer finger together to grab a block, move your hand to carry it, and open your fingers to let it go. Copy the picture as fast as you can.

    You won't see your video, only your digital hand. The video stays on this device, and nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Ask a grown-up before you turn on a camera.
