    Read this book with your hand

    This page uses computer vision: an AI looks through your camera, finds 21 points on your hand, and works out which gesture you are making. Swipe to turn the page, point to scroll, pinch to change the text size.

    You won't see your video, only your digital hand. The video stays on this device, and nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Ask a grown-up before you turn on a camera.
