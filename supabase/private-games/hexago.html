Controls and rules

How to play

8
The number is how many landings are leftEach landing takes one. The colour changes as it counts down, from magenta 8 to red 1.
1
Red is the last landingLanding on red is safe. When you bounce off it, it cracks and falls away.
G
Grey tiles never run outUse them to stop and plan. A flag tile is a checkpoint.
W
White tiles throw the ballTo one of the marked spots, picked at random. You can't steer while flying.
Diamonds fill the rescue meterSmall ones add 1, big ones 3. At 10, missing a tile makes the ball hover so you can steer to safety.
P
Reach the glowing gateLand on the portal to finish the level.
H
Hills and rampsHop up one step at a time; two steps up is a wall you bonk off. Ramps roll you downhill.
T
TrampolinesA big bounce two tiles the way you steer: over gaps and up to high ledges.
S
Switches, bridges, gatesLand on a switch to make its bridge appear or its gate open. Same colour, same group.
M
Platforms and bumpersRide moving platforms by bouncing in place. Wait for a bumper's orange bar to drop.
