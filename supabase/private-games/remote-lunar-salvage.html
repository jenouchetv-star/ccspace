Mission briefing

Remote Lunar Salvage

A meteor shower broke our outpost on Pumice Moon. The greenhouse has no power, and the bridge to the spare power cell is folded up.

Drive the repair rover. Use its Arm, its Magnet and its Laser to clear the road, fix the bridge and bring back the power cell. Chike will tell you what he needs next. Scan things to collect Field Notes about real space science.

How to play

DriveTurn the steering wheel and hold GO. Follow the blue beam. You cannot drive while the arm holds something, so put cargo in the cargo bed on the back.
ArmThe rover parks. The joystick swings and lifts the arm, the lever slides it farther or nearer, and the big button grabs. Press AIM (or tap a part) and the arm swings right to it. The ring on the ground shows where the claw is.
MagnetThe same joystick and lever. Hold the big magnet button to pull metal parts up, and let go to drop them. Not every metal sticks.
LaserPut the purple target on a boulder with glowing cracks and hold FIRE. It heats up and cracks open. Tap a boulder, or press AIM, to point the laser at it.
ScanScan a part to see what it is made of and which tool can lift it. New facts go in your Field Notes.
Keys: 1 Drive, 2 Arm, 3 Magnet, 4 Laser. W A S D or arrows to drive and aim. Q and E reach in and out. Space grabs, holds the magnet or fires the laser. G aims. R turns a part. F scans. T tests. P pauses.
