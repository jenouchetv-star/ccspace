Mission briefing

Remote Lunar Salvage

A meteor shower broke our Moon outpost. The greenhouse has no power, and the bridge to the spare power cell is folded up.

Drive the repair rover, use its Arm and Magnet, and figure out which tool works on which part. Scan things to collect Field Notes about real space science.

Keyboard, mouse, touch and game controllers work too.

How to play

DriveSteer the rover to the blue beam. You cannot drive while the arm holds something, so put cargo in the cargo bed on the back.
ArmThe rover parks. Turn the arm, raise or lower it, reach in or out, then Grab. The ring on the ground shows where the claw is.
MagnetHold the Magnet button to pull metal parts up. Let go to drop them. Not every metal sticks.
ScanScan a part to see what it is made of and which tool can lift it. New facts go in your Field Notes.
Keys: 1 Drive, 2 Arm, 3 Magnet. W A S D or arrows to drive and aim. Q and E reach in and out. Space grabs, or holds the magnet. R turns a part. F scans. T tests. P pauses.
