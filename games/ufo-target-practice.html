What's efficiency?

Efficiency is how many of your shots actually hit a UFO, out of all the shots you fired.

Say you fired 10 shots and 7 of them hit a UFO. That's 7 out of 10 — or 70% efficiency.

Shooting fast fires more lasers, but aiming carefully is what makes your efficiency go up!
