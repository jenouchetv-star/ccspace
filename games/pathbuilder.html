Pathbuilder

Your little car is at the start. Build a road, one card at a time, so it can drive all the way home before the cards run out.

  • Pick a card, then pick a spotClick a road card, then click a square next to the end of your road.
  • Line up the openingsRoads only connect edge to edge. Press R or Turn to rotate a card.
  • Bridges go over thingsA bridge can cross a river, sit on the grass, or go over another road. Only bridges can cross water.
  • Stuck? Change your cardsSwap my cards for a new set of random ones, or undo your last card.
  • Fewer cards, more starsThe par shows the shortest way. Can you get home using about that many cards?
