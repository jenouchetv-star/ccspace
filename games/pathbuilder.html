How to Play

Your little car is waiting across the island. Build a road from your house, one card at a time, all the way to the car before the cards run out. When the road reaches it, the car drives the whole road home!

  • StraightCorner
    Pick a card, then pick a spotClick a road card, then click a square next to the end of your road. Your road starts at your house.
  • Try it, turn it, place itClick a square to try the card there, turn it with the arrows (or R), then tap Place. A road is locked in once it is placed.
  • BridgeTunnelRamp
    Bridges, tunnels and rampsA bridge crosses a river or goes over a road. A tunnel goes under a rock. A ramp jumps one rock, one river or one gap in the sea. Trees are protected: go around them.
  • Stuck? Change your cardsSwap my cards for a new set of random ones, or undo your last card. If the road ends, Go back takes you back along your road to a spot where you can build again.
  • Fewer cards, more starsThe par shows the shortest way. Can you get home using about that many cards?
