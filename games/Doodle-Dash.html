How to play

1. Pick a player and a world, then press Launch game.

2. Make a level: draw one on screen, or draw on paper and snap a photo. Colors tell the game what things are: black ground, green start, blue goal, yellow coins, purple power-ups, teal moving platforms, red lava, orange enemies.

3. Run, jump, grab coins and reach the flag. Arrow keys or A and D move, Space or W jumps.

4. Want to play together? Press Share code while you play, and give the 6-character code to a friend. They type it under Join a friend and appear in the same world.
